The full copyright notice and licence text for each family is on its Google
Fonts page (https://fonts.google.com: search "Lilita One", "Fraunces",
"DM Sans").

The .woff2 files here are the Latin, Latin-Extended and Vietnamese subsets as
served by Google Fonts. They are wired up in src/fonts.css.
